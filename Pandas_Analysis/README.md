# 📝 Practice Questions

## Level 1: Basic Statistical Analysis

1. Find the total number of products sold using the `Quantity` column.
2. Find the total sales from the `Sales` column.
3. Find the average sales value.
4. Find the median sales value.
5. Find the maximum sales value.
6. Find the minimum sales value.
7. Find the standard deviation of the `Sales` column.
8. Find the variance of the `Sales` column.
9. Generate a complete statistical summary of all numeric columns.
10. Find the 25th, 50th, and 75th percentiles of the `Sales` column.

---

## Level 2: Unique Values and Frequency Analysis

11. Find all unique cities in the dataset.
12. Count the number of unique customers.
13. Count how many orders came from each city.
14. Find the percentage distribution of orders by city.
15. Count how many times each product appears in the dataset.
16. Find the most frequently purchased product based on the number of rows.
17. Find the least frequently purchased product based on the number of rows.
18. Count the number of unique products in each category.
19. Find the most common category in the dataset.
20. Find how many orders each customer has placed.

---

## Level 3: Filtering and Conditional Analysis

21. Display all orders where sales are greater than 50,000.
22. Find all orders where the quantity purchased is greater than 2.
23. Display all orders from Delhi.
24. Find all orders where the city is Delhi and sales are greater than 30,000.
25. Find all orders where the category is Electronics or Furniture.
26. Find all products with a price between 10,000 and 40,000.
27. Find all orders where the quantity is not equal to 1.
28. Find all orders from selected cities using `.isin()`.
29. Find all rows where sales are above the average sales of the entire dataset.
30. Find all orders where the sales value is below the median sales value.

---

## Level 4: Creating Columns for Analysis

31. If the dataset contains `Price` and `Quantity`, create a new column called `Sales`.
32. Create a column called `Discounted_Price` by applying a 10% discount to the price.
33. Create a column that categorizes each order as `"High Sales"` if sales are greater than or equal to 50,000; otherwise `"Low Sales"`.
34. Create three sales categories: `Low`, `Medium`, and `High` based on sales values.
35. Create a column showing the percentage contribution of each order to total sales.
36. Create a column that ranks orders based on sales, where the highest sale receives rank 1.

---

## Level 5: Sorting and Finding Extremes

37. Sort the dataset by sales from highest to lowest.
38. Display the top 5 orders with the highest sales.
39. Display the bottom 5 orders with the lowest sales.
40. Find the complete row containing the highest sales value.
41. Find the complete row containing the lowest sales value.
42. Find the top 3 most expensive products based on price.

---

## Level 6: GroupBy Analysis

43. Calculate total sales generated by each city.
44. Calculate the average sales generated in each city.
45. Find the total quantity sold for each product.
46. Calculate total sales for each category.
47. Find the average price of products in each category.
48. For each city, calculate:

* Total Sales
* Average Sales
* Maximum Sales
* Minimum Sales

49. Calculate total sales for every combination of city and category.
50. Find the top-performing city based on total sales and identify how much it contributed as a percentage of overall sales.

# 🧠 Learning Objective

The goal of these exercises is not just to memorize Pandas functions.

The main objective is to develop the ability to break a business question into smaller analytical steps.

A useful problem-solving approach is:

```text
Understand the Question
        ↓
Identify Required Columns
        ↓
Filter the Data (if required)
        ↓
Group the Data (if required)
        ↓
Perform Calculations
        ↓
Sort the Results
        ↓
Extract Insights
```
